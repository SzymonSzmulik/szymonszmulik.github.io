Reseller Desk on your phone

Install this once and Reseller Desk gets its own icon on your home screen and in your app list. Tapping it opens your own Reseller Desk in Claude.

1. Get the plugin first

This installer only opens your own Reseller Desk, and that comes from the plugin. In Claude, open Customize › Plugins, then Add › Add marketplace. Paste SzymonSzmulik/reseller-desk, turn on Sync automatically, then tap Add on Reseller Desk. Then start a new chat and say "open reseller desk and pin it".

Open Plugins

2. Your Reseller Desk link

In Claude, open your Reseller Desk, tap Share, then Copy link. Paste it here. It's saved only on this phone.

Open Reseller Desk

3. Install it

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (not "Add shortcut"), then Install.
  4. Find Reseller Desk on your home screen and in your app list.

Good to know

The icon opens your Reseller Desk in Claude, so Chrome may show a thin bar with claude.ai at the top. That bar comes from Chrome, because your app lives in Claude. Everything stays private to you.

To change your link later, open this page in Chrome and paste the new one.
