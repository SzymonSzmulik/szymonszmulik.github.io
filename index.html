Everything a reseller tracks, in one place

01Listings from photos

Title, item specifics, condition and description written for you. Copy and paste into eBay or Vinted.

02Stock with SKUs

Every item gets a box and a number that's never reused, so you can find it on the shelf in seconds.

03Real profit

Sales, fees, postage, entry fees and expenses add up to what you actually made, month by month.

04Miles you can claim

Log trips to carboots and auctions. The HMRC claim is worked out for you and goes into your expenses.

05Your own spreadsheet

Everything lives in a Google Sheet you own. The app adapts to your columns and keeps your dropdowns up to date.

06Group chat

Talk to other resellers in the app, see who's online and reply to messages.

Get started in three steps

  1. Make your own copy of the free tracker (the "Get the free tracker" button). It's yours, in your Google Drive.
  2. Open the app in Claude and connect Google Drive and Google Sheets when it asks.
  3. Paste your tracker link into the app. It matches your columns and you're ready to list.

Reseller Desk runs inside Claude (claude.ai), so you'll need a Claude account. Your data stays in your own Google Sheet; nobody else can see it.
