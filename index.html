What it does

The whole resale round, in one place.

Reseller Desk sits on top of your own Google Sheet. Add an item, log a sale or an expense, and it writes the row for you, so your numbers are always right.

Listings from photos

Add photos and a note. You get the title, item specifics, condition and a ready-to-paste description.

Your real profit

What buyers paid, minus what you paid, fees, postage and expenses. By month, by site and by place you buy.

Stock that sells

See what's waiting longest and what's tied up in each box, so you know what to drop or relist.

Every place you buy

Carboots, charity shops, Facebook Marketplace, auctions. It learns where you buy and asks about entry fees.

Every place you sell

eBay, Vinted, Facebook and more, with a check against each app so the numbers match.

A group chat

Talk to other resellers using the app. Swap finds, prices and carboot tips.

Tell it what to track

Describe something new, like a warehouse tab or a repair cost. It asks questions if it needs more, shows you the change first, adds the tab, column and Home widget, and has an Undo.

Repairs and entry fees

Costs for fixing an item come off its profit. A place you pay to get into can add its usual fee for you on the day you visit.

Your whole spreadsheet

Look through every tab of your sheet inside the app and change rows there. Formula columns stay locked.

On your phone

Built for the car park, not the office.

Big buttons, quick taps, and widgets you can move around like your phone's home screen.

Get started

Up and running in five minutes.

  1. Get your tracker

    Make your own copy of the free tracker. It's yours, in your Google Drive.

    Get the free tracker

  2. Add the plugin

    In Claude, open Customize › Plugins, then Add › Add marketplace. Paste SzymonSzmulik/reseller-desk, turn on Sync automatically, then tap Add on Reseller Desk.

    Open Plugins

  3. Open the app

    Start a new chat and say "open reseller desk and pin it". Paste your tracker link into it and you're set up.

Connect Google Drive and Google Sheets in Claude if you haven't already. On Android? Open the Android installer in Chrome to make it a real app on your home screen. Works on free and paid Claude plans; free plans have lower usage limits.

Questions

Before you start.

Who made it?

Claude. The whole app was vibe coded with Claude.

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free. It runs on your own Claude account, so listing writing counts towards your Claude plan's usage like any other chat. There's nothing to pay us.

Where's my data kept?

In your own Google Sheet and Google Drive. The app reads and writes your sheet; we never hold your stock or sales.

I already have a spreadsheet. Can I keep it?

Yes. When you add it, the app reads your columns, guesses what each one is and asks you about anything unclear. Nothing changes in your sheet until you agree.

Does it work for Vinted and Facebook Marketplace?

Yes. Add the places you sell, and the app tracks profit by site and gives you a check against each app. Facebook doesn't show totals, so you get a list to tick off instead.

Do I have to use SKUs and boxes?

No. Both are optional. Turn SKUs off, or use them without boxes and they're just a number.

Does it work on a computer too?

Yes. On a computer the widgets spread out so your whole dashboard fits on one screen. Phone and computer keep their own layouts.
